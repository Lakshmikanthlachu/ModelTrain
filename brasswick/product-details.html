Modelled from works drawings and photographs of the preserved prototype, the Pacific captures the long boiler, elegant splashers and lined livery of the golden age of express steam.

A die-cast chassis puts weight where it matters, and the skew-wound motor with twin flywheels delivers crawl-speed shunting as well as a convincing 90 mph scale top speed.

FAQs

Before you buy.

Will it run on my existing track?
Yes, on any HO/OO code 75, 83 or 100 track with a minimum radius of 438 mm. Code 70 requires the optional low-profile wheel set.
Can I add sound later?
The 21-pin interface accepts any MTC decoder. Our workshop can fit and program a sound decoder for $129 including the decoder.
How is it packaged?
In a foam-lined display case inside a double-walled shipping box. We test-run every locomotive for 30 minutes before packing.
What's the warranty?
Two years on the motor, gearbox and electronics. Wear items such as traction tyres are covered for six months.
