Our philosophy

A good layout is not a toy, it is a place. Every hedgerow, every lamp post and every timetable is chosen so the trains feel like they are going somewhere that matters.

What we do

Every discipline under one workshop roof.

Track planning & design

Scale drawings, 3D previews and operating sessions on paper before a single board is cut.

DCC & automation

Sound, block detection and computer-controlled timetables.

Scenery & landscapes

Hand-made trees, cast rock faces and resin water.

Structures & lighting

Scratch-built buildings from your photos, lit with warm micro LEDs and a programmable dusk cycle.

Weathering & detailing

Rolling stock, buildings and track aged to match your chosen era and region.

Relocation & restoration

We dismantle, move and revive inherited or older layouts.

Exhibition hire

Modular show layouts, crewed by our team, for events and museums.

How a build runs

Four stations from first call to first train.

A typical Club Layout takes twelve to sixteen weeks. You'll always know which station we're at.

Read the full process
  1. 1Week 1

    Survey & brief

    A two-hour visit (or video call) to measure the space, photograph access routes and understand your era, region and operating style.

  2. 2Weeks 2–3

    Track plan & fixed quote

    You receive a scale track plan, a 3D preview and a fixed quote. Two revision rounds are included.

  3. 3Weeks 4–12

    Build, wire & test

    Baseboards, track and DCC built in our workshop with weekly photo updates. Every layout completes 40 hours of test running.

  4. 4Weeks 10–16

    Scenic finish & install

    Landscape, structures and lighting, then delivery, installation and an operating lesson for the whole family.

Questions

Things clients ask before the first call.

Do you build layouts outside Oregon?
Yes. About half of our builds are delivered across the US and Canada in sectional form. For international projects we ship in custom crates and can fly a builder out for installation.
Can you incorporate my existing locomotives and stock?
Absolutely. We audit your collection during the survey, and can service, re-motor or fit DCC sound decoders so everything runs reliably on the new layout.
What happens if something breaks after delivery?
Every layout carries a two-year running guarantee covering track, wiring and electronics. Care plans extend that with annual servicing visits.
How much space do I need?
Less than you might think. A shelf switcher can live on a 2.4 m bookcase; an N scale continuous run fits comfortably in 3 × 1.5 m.
Can I watch the build?
Yes — you'll get weekly photo updates and are welcome to visit the workshop by appointment.
