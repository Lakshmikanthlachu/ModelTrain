Custom layout building

From a sketch on a napkin to a railway that runs itself.

Our workshop has delivered 640 layouts — from bookshelf shunting puzzles to museum centrepieces. Fixed quotes, weekly photo updates and a two-year running guarantee.

  1. 01

    Survey & brief

    We measure your space and talk eras, railways and how you like to operate.

  2. 02

    Track plan

    A scale drawing and 3D preview, refined until every siding earns its place.

  3. 03

    Build & wire

    Baseboards, track and DCC built in our workshop, tested for 40 running hours.

  4. 04

    Scenic finish

    Landscape, structures, lighting and weathering — then delivered and installed.
