Pricing FAQ

Fares & tickets, explained.

Can I cancel my membership?
Any time, from your account. Monthly plans end at the close of the billing month; annual plans are refunded pro-rata.
Do member discounts apply to custom layouts?
Engine Shed and Signal Box members receive 5% off the build price of any layout package.
What is workshop bench time?
A reserved bench in our workshop, with tools, airbrush booth and test track, plus advice from staff.
Are build package prices fixed?
The prices shown are typical starting points. After a free survey you receive a fixed, itemised quote.
