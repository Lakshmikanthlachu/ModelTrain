Our story

Four decades of small trains and big ideas.

A family shop that grew into a workshop — still on the same street, still opening the doors at ten every Tuesday.

38

years at 14 Signal Box Lane

Since 1987

It started with one shelf of second-hand locomotives.

Arthur Penhallow opened Brasswick in a former ticket office, selling and repairing locomotives that other shops turned away. Word spread among local clubs, and by the mid-nineties the back room had become a layout workshop.

Today we're a team of nine: retail specialists, electronics engineers, scenic artists and designers. We still repair what others won't, and we still believe the best layouts are built around the stories their owners want to tell.

Arthur Penhallow

Founder & Master Builder

Mission & values

What keeps the lamps lit.

Our mission

To make railway modelling more welcoming — with honest advice, fair prices and layouts that run as well as they look.

Craft first

Every locomotive is test-run before it leaves the shop. Every layout is run for forty hours before it leaves the workshop.

Community

We host a free club night every Thursday, sponsor two junior modelling clubs and lend layouts to local schools.

History

Stations along the line.

  1. 1987

    First shelf

    Brasswick opens in a former ticket office with 140 second-hand locomotives.

  2. 1994

    The back room

    Our first commissioned layout — a 9 m branch line for the Portland Model Railway Club.

  3. 2003

    DCC arrives

    We become one of the first shops in the Pacific Northwest to offer sound decoder fitting.

  4. 2011

    Workshop expansion

    A dedicated 400 m² building for baseboards, scenery and electronics.

  5. 2019

    Museum work

    Our Alpine Viaducts diorama opens at the Rail Heritage Trust.

  6. 2026

    Layout Studio

    Nine builders, 640 layouts delivered and an online store shipping worldwide.
